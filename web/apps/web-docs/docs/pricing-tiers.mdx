## Trial System

Every Igris Inertial account is eligible for one 7-day free trial on any tier.

**How it works:**

1. Select a tier (Seed, Horizon, or Infinite) when you start your trial.
2. Your trial runs for 7 days from the activation date.
3. At the end of the trial, you can subscribe to continue at the selected tier.
4. If you do not subscribe before the trial ends, your account moves to the Seed tier. You are not locked out. All data and runtime configuration is preserved.

**Key details:**
- No credit card is required to start a trial.
- One trial per account — you cannot start a second trial after the first has ended.
- If you start a Horizon or Infinite trial and choose not to subscribe, the Seed tier remains active so your work is not interrupted.

## Billing

**Payment processor:** All billing is handled by [Polar.sh](https://polar.sh). You will be redirected to a Polar.sh checkout page when subscribing.

**Billing cycle:** Monthly. There are no annual contracts or long-term commitments.

**Cancellation:** Cancel anytime from the billing section of your console account at [console.igrisinertial.com](https://console.igrisinertial.com).

**No overage charges:** Igris Inertial does not charge per-request fees or overage rates. Your subscription covers your runtime instance capacity. If you need more instances, upgrade to a higher tier.

## Upgrading and Downgrading

**Upgrading:** You can upgrade your tier at any time from the billing settings in the console. The new tier's features and instance capacity become available immediately. Charges are prorated for the remainder of the billing period.

**Downgrading:** Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Your current tier's features and capacity remain active until then.

**Instance limits:** If your active runtime count exceeds the limit of a lower tier after a downgrade, the excess instances will be deregistered at the billing cycle boundary. You will receive an email notification before this happens.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What counts as a runtime instance?

A runtime instance is a single deployed Igris runtime process registered to your account. Each instance can run independently, participate in fleet coordination, and report health and telemetry back to the console. The instance count is the primary dimension that determines your tier.

### Can I run multiple instances per machine?

Yes. There is no restriction on how many instances run on a single host. The limit applies to the total number of registered instances across your entire fleet.

### What happens to my data if I do not subscribe after the trial?

Your account moves to the Seed tier. Audit logs, fleet configuration, and runtime history are preserved within the Seed tier's 30-day log retention window. You are not locked out, and you can subscribe at any time to restore higher-tier features.

### Can I start on Seed and upgrade later?

Yes. Upgrades are self-service and take effect immediately. If you start on Seed and later need shadow mode, speculative execution, or a larger fleet, upgrade to Horizon or Infinite from the billing settings in the console.

### Is there a setup fee or onboarding cost?

No setup fee on any tier. Infinite includes dedicated onboarding as part of the subscription at no additional cost.

### How does on-premise deployment work on Infinite?

On Infinite, you can deploy the Igris runtime within your own infrastructure — including air-gapped or private cloud environments. The runtime binary is distributed through your authenticated account and can be installed on any supported Linux or macOS target. The fleet dashboard and telemetry still communicate with the Igris Inertial cloud control plane unless you configure a self-hosted endpoint.
